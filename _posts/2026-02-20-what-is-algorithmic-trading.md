<p>Algorithmic trading — also called algo trading or automated trading — is the use of computer programs to execute buy and sell orders in financial markets based on a predefined set of rules. These rules can be as simple as "buy when the 50-day moving average crosses above the 200-day moving average," or as complex as machine-learning models that analyse hundreds of variables in real time.</p><h2>Why It Matters</h2><p>Today, algorithmic trading accounts for the majority of volume on major exchanges worldwide. Institutional players — hedge funds, banks, market makers — have used it for decades to gain speed, consistency, and scale advantages over manual traders.</p><p>The good news: the tools are no longer exclusive to Wall Street. Platforms like MetaTrader 4/5, cTrader, and NinjaTrader have made professional-grade algorithmic trading accessible to retail traders around the world.</p><h2>Key Advantages</h2><ul><li><strong>Speed:</strong> Algorithms execute in milliseconds — far faster than any human.</li><li><strong>Consistency:</strong> No emotions. The algo follows its rules every single time.</li><li><strong>Backtesting:</strong> You can test a strategy against years of historical data before risking real money.</li><li><strong>24/7 Operation:</strong> Your bot trades while you sleep, capturing opportunities around the clock.</li></ul><h2>Getting Started</h2><p>The first step is choosing a platform. MT4 and MT5 are the most widely used, with a huge ecosystem of Expert Advisors (EAs) and indicators. cTrader is popular for its clean API and cloud deployment. NinjaTrader excels for futures traders who need advanced order-flow tools.</p><p>Once you've chosen a platform, the next step is defining a strategy — either by learning to code one yourself, or working with a professional developer.
