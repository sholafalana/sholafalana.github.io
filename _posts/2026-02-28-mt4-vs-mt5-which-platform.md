<p>MetaTrader 4 (MT4) and MetaTrader 5 (MT5) are the two most popular retail trading platforms in the world. Both are made by MetaQuotes, but they are not simply different versions of the same thing — they were built with different goals in mind.</p><h2>MetaTrader 4</h2><p>MT4 was released in 2005 and quickly became the de-facto standard for forex trading. Its strengths are simplicity, stability, and an enormous library of existing Expert Advisors and indicators.</p><ul><li>Forex-focused (4 pending order types)</li><li>MQL4 language — easier to learn</li><li>Huge existing EA/indicator marketplace</li><li>Lower resource usage</li></ul><h2>MetaTrader 5</h2><p>MT5 was released in 2010 as a multi-asset platform designed for stocks, futures, and forex alike. It's more powerful than MT4, with a superior backtesting engine, more order types, and a more modern language (MQL5).</p><ul><li>Multi-asset: forex, stocks, futures, options</li><li>6 pending order types</li><li>MQL5 — more powerful, object-oriented</li><li>Faster backtester with multi-threaded optimisation</li><li>Economic calendar and market depth built in</li></ul><h2>Which Should You Choose?</h2><p>Choose <strong>MT4</strong> if you trade forex exclusively, already have existing MQL4 EAs, or prefer simplicity. Choose <strong>MT5</strong> if you trade multiple asset classes, want faster backtesting, or are starting from scratch.</p>
